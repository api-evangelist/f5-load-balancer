F5 is partnering with SeattleFWC26 and Black Players for Change to create community impact around the 2026 FIFA World Cup in Seattle. The effort includes sending 25 high school students to a match on Juneteenth and supporting 27 Black-owned businesses through the Unity Loop initiative.
