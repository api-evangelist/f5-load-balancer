Enterprises now operate an average of seven AI models simultaneously, with 93% running hybrid multicloud environments. F5 and Equinix are partnering to address distributed AI governance by combining the Equinix Distributed AI Hub and F5 AI Guardrails, shifting AI inference and inspection closer to the edge to optimize performance and minimize latency.
