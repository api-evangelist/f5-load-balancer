AI inference and agent systems behave unpredictably compared to traditional services, making incomplete observability a critical operational challenge; without visibility into token generation, model pathways, and execution lineage, teams blame the wrong component or scale the wrong resource. The fix requires instrumentation aligned with AI behavior, including execution tracing for agent workflows, cost and latency as primary metrics, and a high-speed telemetry plane operating at sub-millisecond speeds.
